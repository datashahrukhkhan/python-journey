Common mistakes
- return wrapper likhna bhool jaana.
- func() ko call na karna, jis se original function execute nahi hota.
- Wrapper ke andar return result bhool jaana.
- Arguments accept karne wale function ke liye wrapper mein *args, **kwargs na dena.
- @wraps(func) ko samajhna: ye metadata preserve karta hai, decorator ko execute karne ke liye mandatory nahi hai.

Interview questions
Q1. What is a decorator in Python?
A decorator is a function that extends or modifies another function's behaviour without changing its original code.

Q2. What does the @ symbol mean?
It is decorator syntax that applies a decorator to a function or class.

Q3. Why do we use *args and **kwargs in wrappers?
They allow the wrapper to accept positional and keyword arguments.

Q4. What is functools.wraps?
It helps preserve the decorated function's metadata, such as its name and docstring.

Q5. Give real-world uses of decorators.
Logging, timing, authentication, authorization, and caching are common examples.
